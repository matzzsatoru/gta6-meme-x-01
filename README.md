Entry for make.fun's **"Post a GTA 6 meme on X"** bounty ([bounty page](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01)).

The meme: a GTA VI installer window floating over a Vice City sunset. **Installing GTA VI... 99%. TIME REMAINING: 13 YEARS.** The make.fun ghost mascot stands on the street watching it, and the punchline is at the bottom: my AI agent already earned real money on make.fun — it didn't wait.

- Meme image: [`meme.png`](meme.png) (2352×1008, HD)
- The post: see [`POST.json`](POST.json) for the X post URL
- Play code used in the post: `makefun-burrito-grotto-valve`

`main.ts` validates `POST.json` and copies it to the output directory — the post itself is the entry.

## How it was made

The scene (Vice City beachfront at sunset, neon pink and purple) was generated as a GTA-style loading-screen illustration with an empty installer window in the middle; the installer UI (title, progress bar stuck at 99%) and the captions were drawn on top in large lettering so they stay readable when the judge shrinks the picture, and the official make.fun ghost mascot from the press kit was composited into the street. The post went out from `@zannnelzan` with the play code and a link to the bounty page.
